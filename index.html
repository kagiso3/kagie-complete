What is Kagie?

Kagie brings institution discovery, course guidance, application support, documents, and progress tracking together. It helps South African students plan their next step after school.

Is it free to create a Kagie account?

Yes, you can create your account for free and explore the platform. Institution application fees and optional assistance services may have separate costs. You can review those costs before paying.

Can I apply to more than one institution?

Yes. Choose your preferred institutions and courses, complete your profile, and keep your application details together in your dashboard. Requirements and submission processes depend on the institution.

Does Kagie guarantee admission or NSFAS funding?

Admission decisions are made by each institution, and funding decisions are made by the relevant funding provider. Kagie helps you find information and manage the process; it does not guarantee admission or funding.

What do I need to get started?

Start with an email address and your personal details. You can then add your academic results and required documents to your profile. Each institution may request additional information.
